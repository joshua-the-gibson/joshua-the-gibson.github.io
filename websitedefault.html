The Hallowed Court

House Rosehallow within the Hallowed Court

A call for the North

In recent years, stability has once again begun to take root in the Highlands, a land once marked by endless strife and cunning combatants. With this new peace comes the promise of opportunity. In the northern frontier, a resolute hand and a sharp mind can carve one's name into the annals of history, uniting the north under one banner, and ensuring that the region thrives once again.

But the drums of war beat ever louder in the north. Even now, threats gather on the borders, preying upon the newfound comforts of the city. Troll tribes encroach upon the Highlands, while bandits and cutthroats ravage trade routes to the west. In the heart of the city, internal strife constantly stirs beneath the surface. And beyond the Highlands, forgotten lands still remain. Lands lost to the human kingdom, but never abandoned in spirit.

It is in the spirit of preserving what we hold dear, and claiming what is rightfully ours, that the Hallowed Court spreads its wings and beats its own drum.

We seek individuals of all backgrounds noble and humble alike offering ample opportunities to rise in status, as well as rewards of coin and land for those who show loyalty and fervour. Oaths are not required upon first meeting. Trust must be earned by both sides, and only when that trust is solidified will the time come for binding oaths. For an oath to the Court is a sacred bond, one in which the Court swears to serve your interests just as you pledge to serve its cause.

Join us, and together we will reshape the north.

What to expect from us

At the Hallowed Court, we craft events and socials with meticulous attention to detail, always considering the unique desires of our players. Character development is at the heart of what we do. Delving deep into your character’s story, motivations, and growth is an experience that can often surprise you, the player, revealing new layers and potential you never imagined.

We pride ourselves on a vibrant, inclusive community welcoming and accepting of all. We understand that Out of Character (OOC) and In Character (IC) can be vastly different, and we believe that open communication is the foundation of any good community, both in and out of the game. Our guild is a sanctuary, a place where you can escape the stress and drama of the outside world, and truly immerse yourself in a space where you can get lost in adventure and fire-side tales.

Expect military-based actions that test your courage and strategic thinking, but also offer the thrill of intrigue and the possibility of failure. It is only through loss that we truly learn and grow. Your actions will shape the outcomes of battles, teaching you more about your character’s heart and what they are truly capable of (or not).

We also offer a slower pace for those who prefer it where combat doesn’t define every moment. Whether you're a farrier tending to a knight's steed, a chef traveling with the caravan to feed the hungry, or a courier delivering important messages, there is a place for everyone. No matter your role, there is purpose and meaning waiting for you.

Hierarchy within the Court

Our structure:

  • Lord / Lady – Reserved for the highest leaders, these titles would reflect their noble standing within the Court.
  • Warden – A title for those responsible with the defence of northern lands and interests, a right-hand.
  • Chancellor – For those with a seat at the table.
  • Knight – A man or woman of prowess, bound to the land and her people.
  • Oathbound – Look no further for loyalty than the oathbound, reserved for those that have earnt the trust and responsibility.
  • Man at Arms - For the bread and butter of the standing force, encompassing all manners of roles and responsibilites on the field and off it.
  • Acolyte – A position held by those new to the Court, a trial of sorts.
